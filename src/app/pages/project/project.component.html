About the Project

NeuroScan AI is a deep learning based system designed to classify brain MRI scans into four categories: Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary and No Tumor.

The system leverages state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks to deliver accurate, fast and reliable predictions to assist radiologists in clinical decision making.
